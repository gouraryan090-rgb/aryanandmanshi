💙 His Expectations

I don't have a lot of expectations. I just want you to always be a part of my life. ❤️

I want you to understand me, even when I can't express everything in words. Sometimes I struggle to explain how I feel, but I hope you'll always try to understand what's in my heart. 🫶

I hope we always have trust between us. No matter what the situation is, please never lie to me. The truth might hurt for a while, but a lie hurts much more. ❤️

And if I ever do something that upsets you or makes you feel bad, please tell me directly. Don't keep it to yourself. I may make mistakes, but I'll always try my best to understand them and become a better person for us. 🥹

That's all I really wish for. No matter what life brings, I hope we never stop choosing each other, never stop understanding each other, and never stop fighting for us. ❤️
